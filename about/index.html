About Us

Who We Are

Home Expert Finders Realty Inc.

“Magka-BAHAY Every JUAN”

Home Expert Finders Realty Inc. is an award-winning real estate company dedicated to helping Filipinos achieve their dream of homeownership. With nearly three decades of experience, we have guided thousands of families in finding not just houses—but homes where their futures begin.

Founded in 1996 as RMDC Realty, our journey started with a simple mission: to help families find a place they can truly call their own. Through perseverance, faith, and hard work, we grew into what is now HOME EXPERT FINDERS REALTY INC., a trusted name in real estate.

Behind this success is a story of passion and purpose. Founded by Mrs. Marilou Dela Cruz and Mr. Rolando Dela Cruz, the company reflects a powerful journey—from humble beginnings as a sales agent to building a corporation rooted in service, integrity, and care.

Today, we proudly celebrate nearly 30 years of service, continuing to transform lives by making homeownership accessible, affordable, and meaningful for every Filipino family.
